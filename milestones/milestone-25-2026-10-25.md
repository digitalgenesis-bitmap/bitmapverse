## Closure scope and limits

**CLOSED / PASS** means the reported chain was completed: accepted publication,
independent Name-tag discovery, matching artifact hash, BIP-322 verification
and current-holder validation at the recorded historical tip.

- Nostr transports untrusted candidates. Its signature authenticates the
  publisher; Bitcoin/BIP-322 evidence authorizes the binding.
- Kind `1` uses `d` as a lookup key. This milestone establishes no replacement,
  supersession, revocation, latest-event selection or complete-history semantics.
  A newer Nostr timestamp is not binding authority.
- Relay acceptance and one successful retrieval do not guarantee retention,
  future availability, persistence, uptime or complete discovery. The observed
  handshake failures remain part of the evidence.
- GitHub availability is a retrieval dependency. A pinned commit and matching
  digest identify exact bytes; they do not guarantee permanent hosting.
- Historical holder validation is scoped to its observed Bitcoin state. It is
  not a perpetual assertion of ownership and must be checked again when used.
- This milestone covers only `bitmapverse.bitmap → 937336.bitmap`. The existing
  `experiments/experiment-25/README.md` concerns separate multi-Name work and
  retains its own `CLOSED / ACCEPT LOCAL` status.

This documentation performs no new experiment, Nostr signing or publication,
Bitcoin operation, code change, test change or deployment. A second relay is
outside this closure and requires a separate operational decision.
