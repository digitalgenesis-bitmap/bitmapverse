## 5. Deterministic verification methodology

`evaluateHistoricalOwnership()` runs offline, reads no clock, and fails closed.

1. Require exactly the 12 pinned evidence files, and check each SHA-256.
2. Parse each provider's responses strictly, and require both providers to agree.
3. Require the block hash at 972169 to equal the signed snapshot hash.
4. Require the transaction to be confirmed at a height ≤ 972169, and the
   capture tip to be > 972169.
5. Require output 15 to be unspent at the capture tip (a spent output is
   rejected, because no spend height was captured).
6. Parse the raw transaction independently and recompute the txid from the
   non-witness serialization.
7. Derive output 15's P2TR address with a local bech32m encoder, cross-checked
   against the unchanged Experiment #1 `p2trScriptPubKey`.
8. Check that the archived record places the District's sat at
   `75918e86…:15:0` with the same address and creation height, that the
   pinned ledger provenance rows are present, and that the observation height
   (959531) lies inside the unspent window.

Reasoning: the output was created at 942807 and was unspent at 973731, so it
was unspent throughout that window. A sat cannot leave an unspent output, so
the sat observed in `:15` at 959531 was still there at 972169.

- The inscription-to-output association comes from an **archived two-indexer
  observation**.
- The original raw indexer responses were **not preserved**.
- **No independently reconstructed ordinal sat-flow.**
- **No locally validated Bitcoin full node**: headers, proof-of-work and
  merkle inclusion were not checked.
- Bitcoin provider agreement is **corroboration, not independent consensus
  validation**.
- Historical ownership at block 972169 is **not proof of current ownership**.
- The Experiment #23 signature authenticates its original **Name-binding
  message**, not a delegated build grant.
- Experiment #28 remains **`TEST_KEY_FIXTURE`** and **`LOCAL_ONLY`**.
- **No production-ready permission system** has been demonstrated.
